About Luna Café

Our story, our passion, our community.

A Place Born from Campus Life

Luna Café started as a simple idea among students of Don Mariano Marcos Memorial State University – North La Union Campus: create a warm, welcoming space where students and locals could gather, study, and enjoy great coffee.

What began as a small pop-up during campus events has grown into a beloved neighborhood café. We believe that good coffee and genuine conversations can make any day better.

Today, we continue to serve the DMMMSU community and the people of North La Union with the same heart and commitment that started it all.
